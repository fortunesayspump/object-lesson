# Research and design decisions

Checked 17 November 2026.

- The [Met collection record](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436535) establishes the catalog hierarchy and distinguishes record facts from explanatory interpretation. The kit adopts image-first viewing, clear object identity, accession and provenance links, without copying institutional branding or prose.
- The [Met Open Access policy](https://www.metmuseum.org/policies/image-resources) and [API object](https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1/objects/436535) establish permission to use this particular image and its core data. No current room, attendance or audience metrics are inferred.
- [Harvard's typography guidance](https://designsystem.harvardsites.harvard.edu/typography-options) documents Source Sans Pro as a readable academic family. This implementation uses its Source Sans 3 successor, locally bundled for the web and embedded for PDF/PNG. Email falls back to Arial/sans-serif.
- Official [challenge requirements](https://github.com/hyper-elements/hyper-elements/discussions/209) and existing submissions were reviewed. A collection-learning resource was chosen instead of another promotional launch/receipt template. No exhaustive uniqueness claim is made.

## Palette roles

- Ink `#192C34`: primary text and viewer background.
- Cobalt `#174F8A`: links, focus and actionable controls.
- Muted slate `#54656C`: supporting catalog text.
- Cool white `#FFFFFF`: main viewing surface.
- Mist `#EAF0F2`: private writing surface and collection card.
- Rule `#BECBD1`: functional divisions and response lines, never a status-only signal.

The painting supplies visual richness. The interface stays quiet, structured and uncropped. Four formats serve different jobs, not four identical frames: close looking, educator briefing, handwritten response and compact reference.

## Original authorship and limitations

The three prompts were written for this kit. They do not reproduce or adapt a third-party worksheet or branded thinking routine. There is no answer key, generated learner response, invented study result or claim that this activity improves learning. The snapshot can age; verify current museum facts before updating it.
